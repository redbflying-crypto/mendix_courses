Overview

Mendix is a low-code platform for building and shipping business apps quickly. The certification program has three tiers, and you take them in order. Each tier is a prerequisite for the next.

EntryRapid DeveloperNewcomers, business analysts and developers starting out on Mendix.
MidIntermediate DeveloperPeople who have built Mendix apps and want to go further.
ExpertAdvanced DeveloperExperienced developers and architects working on large, enterprise-grade apps.

1. Mendix Rapid Developer

The entry-level certificate. It covers the platform basics so you can start building simple apps.

Who it suits

  • Business users and analysts who want to understand how Mendix works
  • Developers who are new to Mendix
  • Anyone who wants to build small low-code apps

How to prepare

  • Complete the Academy path Become a Rapid Developer.
  • Optionally, add AI and GenAI Fundamentals for background on AI in Mendix.
  • Practise in Studio Pro, using the Mendix documentation.
  • Optionally, take an instructor-led training.
  • Read the Rapid exam FAQ (download .docx).

2. Mendix Intermediate Developer

For people with hands-on Mendix experience who want a deeper understanding of the platform. Rapid certification is required first.

Who it suits

  • Developers who have already built Mendix apps
  • IT professionals growing their low-code skills
  • Analysts who want to add to their Mendix knowledge

How to prepare

  • Work through these Academy paths: Unlocking True Collaboration, Constrain Your Data Using XPath, Create an App with Advanced Page Building, Building an Event App Using Microflows and Expand Your Domain Modeling Skills.
  • Optionally, try Create Your First Agent to build an AI agent in Mendix.
  • Build and deploy a real app end to end.
  • Get involved in the Mendix forum and in training sessions.
  • Optionally, take an instructor-led training.
  • Read the Intermediate exam guide (download .docx).

3. Mendix Advanced Developer

The top tier, for proving expert-level skill. Intermediate certification is required first.

Who it suits

  • Experienced Mendix developers
  • Architects designing complex enterprise apps
  • People responsible for large Mendix rollouts

How to prepare

  • Work through these Academy paths: Advanced Domain Model Skills, Win at Working with Data, Configure Advanced Security, Constrain Your Data Using Advanced XPath, Design and Publish a REST API, Track Application Behavior with Logging, Master Modeling Microflows and Error Handling.
  • Get experience on large, real-world projects.
  • Ask the community to review your solutions.
  • Optionally, take an instructor-led training.
  • Read the Advanced exam guide (download .docx).

Why get certified

The article lists four benefits: you stand out in the low-code job market, the certificates are recognised by employers worldwide, certified developers tend to earn more, and many employers prefer to hire them. The author ends by encouraging readers to start with the Rapid exam and work up through the tiers.

Linked file: Rapid Developer exam FAQ

Word file: mendix-rapid-faqs.docx · Online: view in browser (a Mendix Academy FAQ shared on the blog)

How is the exam structured?
50 questions, five from each of the ten course modules.
Is it timed?
The FAQ says there is no strict time limit and that most candidates take about an hour. The article says 60 minutes.
What kind of questions?
All multiple choice. Questions cover course theory and the hands-on assignments.
Are wrong answers penalised?
No. To pass, you need 75% overall and at least 40% in every module.
Is the course enough on its own?
Yes. The Become a Rapid Developer path is enough, provided you also complete its build assignments (the LearnNow app).
How hard is it?
A little harder than the knowledge checks at the end of each module. If you score around 80% on those checks, you are likely ready. A typical question asks which model element must exist before you can add validation rules (the answer is entities).
Contact
academy@mendix.com

Linked files: Intermediate & Advanced exam guides

Word files: mendix-intermediate-faqs-1.docx and mendix-advanced-faqs.docx · Online: Intermediate, Advanced (Mendix Academy exam guides, v3.0, July 2024). The two guides are almost identical. The differences are listed in the table below.

Registering and access

  • Register with the “Get Certified” button on the certification's page. You can start the exam straight away, or later from My Exam Registrations in the Academy or from the confirmation email.
  • The exam link is valid for 30 days and works for one attempt only. Fees are not refunded if the link expires.
  • The link is personal. If you forward the confirmation email to another address, the link stops working. To use another computer, copy just the link.

Setting up

  • Use an up-to-date OS and Chrome, Firefox or Edge, and run the platform's system compatibility check first. The link to it sits just above the start button.
  • Make sure your internet and power are stable and your webcam works. You must stay on camera the whole time.
  • Use a single monitor in a quiet room, and allow about 15 minutes for setup.

Rules for both exams

  • The exam is proctored, and your screen and webcam are recorded. Recording or copying questions counts as theft.
  • Not allowed: notes, Q&A documents, a second screen or computer, phones and tablets, headphones or earplugs, music, other people in the room, talking or reading questions aloud, chat and meeting apps, and leaving your desk for more than 2 minutes.
  • Clicking “Stop Sharing” ends the attempt immediately, and it counts as a fail.
  • You get up to four warnings. A fifth warning ends the exam automatically.
  • If you break the rules, your result is voided and you are banned from all Mendix exams for three months. Mendix may also tell your employer.

Format, results and retakes

  • 50 multiple-choice questions. You need 75% overall to pass (74% fails), and there is no per-section minimum. Answers are submitted automatically when time runs out.
  • Start with the blue “Start Test” button and end with “Finish Test”. You see a provisional score straight away. The official result arrives by email within three business days after review, and you get a certificate if you pass.
  • You only get your overall and per-section scores. Mendix does not reveal which questions you got wrong.
  • If you fail, you can retake after one week. After later fails, the wait is two weeks. You pay the full fee again for every attempt.
  • If an image is hard to read, click it or change your screen resolution.
  • For technical problems, contact the exam platform's help desk (mettl-support@mercer.com). To report a question you think is wrong, email academy@mendix.com from memory, without screenshots.

Linked pages: Mendix Academy learning paths

These are short summaries of each path the article links to. Module counts and hours come from the Academy catalog as listed in October 2026, where shown. You need a free Mendix account to open the paths themselves.

Become a Rapid Developer Rapid

The core beginner course: build your first low-code apps in Studio Pro. It has 21 modules and takes about 16 hours. academy.mendix.com/link/paths/31

AI and GenAI Fundamentals Rapid

An introduction to the vocabulary of AI, generative AI and AI agents. It has 6 modules. /paths/174

Create Your First Agent Intermediate

A hands-on build of a single AI agent inside a Mendix app, set in an IT helpdesk scenario. You create the agent, give it tools, set up a knowledge base and connect it, then test how the agent behaves. It has 6 modules and takes about 4 hours. You should finish AI and GenAI Fundamentals first, and the exercises need Studio Pro 11.12.1 or later. /paths/175

Unlocking True Collaboration Intermediate

How to work as a team once you start building real company apps in Mendix. It has 5 modules and takes about 2 hours. /paths/61

Constrain Your Data Using XPath Intermediate

Writing XPath constraints to filter and show the data you need. It has 5 modules and takes about 4 hours. /paths/62

Create an App with Advanced Page Building Intermediate

Using page templates to build polished pages such as wizards, reports and a custom login screen. It has 8 modules and takes about 8 hours. /paths/56

Building an Event App Using Microflows Intermediate

Building an event-management app to practise microflow logic. /paths/42

Expand Your Domain Modeling Skills Intermediate

Going beyond basic entities to richer domain models, such as associations and inheritance. /paths/59

Advanced Domain Model Skills Advanced

Designing stable domain models that keep apps fast. It has 8 modules and takes about 6 hours. /paths/106

Win at Working with Data Advanced

How Mendix handles app data and page data sources, with optimization and XPath. It has 5 modules and takes about 4 hours. /paths/3

Configure Advanced Security Advanced

The key parts of Mendix security setup and best practices. It has 6 modules and takes about 4 hours. /paths/9

Constrain Your Data Using Advanced XPath Advanced

More complex XPath queries and functions that build on the Intermediate XPath path. /paths/109

Design and Publish a REST API Advanced

Exposing your app's data and logic as a published REST service. /paths/133

Track Application Behavior with Logging Advanced

Using log nodes and log levels to monitor and troubleshoot running apps. /paths/104

Master Modeling Microflows Advanced

Advanced microflow design, including structure, reuse and performance. /paths/6

Error Handling Advanced

Catching and handling errors in microflows, and how transactions roll back. /paths/81

The Academy catalog had no descriptions for the last seven paths above without signing in. Their summaries are based on the path titles, so check them against the Academy.
